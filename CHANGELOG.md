# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-01)

First release.

- Six-station line: interview, research, hooks, screenplay, arena, ship, with
  a script-guarded gate between every station.
- Eight skills and nine agents: researcher, hook writer, screenwriter, three
  rival managers (Sprint, Fortress, Spark), developer, designer, auditor.
- Engine (Node.js 18+, no dependencies): state and gates, AI-slop detector for
  English, Ukrainian and Russian, online quote verification, acceptance
  checks, clean-room verification of claims, elimination with a graveyard,
  cross-examination with perjury, evidence-backed judging, scoreboard, crown,
  report and a bilingual dashboard.
- Hooks: session-start context and a guard on protected arena files.
- Dogfood run of Skillsmith on itself in `docs/dogfood/`.
- Landing page, four rendered videos, social cards and a launch kit.
