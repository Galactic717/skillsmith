# Hooks: TrialGuard

<!-- ss:platform -->
## Main platform

Platform: Reddit

People in personal finance communities already post about trials they forgot to cancel.

<!-- ss:oneliner -->
## One line

Never pay for a forgotten free trial again.

<!-- ss:hooks -->
## Hooks, strongest first

1. Add a trial in ten seconds; get warned three days before it charges [P].
2. Your bank tells you after the charge. TrialGuard tells you before it [P].
3. No account, no card number, nothing leaves your browser [P].

<!-- ss:onboarding -->
## Words inside the product

- First screen headline: Never pay for a forgotten trial
- Main button: Add trial
- Empty state: No trials yet. Add the one you started today.
- Error message: That end date has already passed. Pick a date from today on.

<!-- ss:launch-post -->
## Launch post

I kept paying for trials I meant to cancel, so I built a tiny site that warns you three days before a free trial charges your card. No account, nothing leaves your browser. What would make you trust a tool like this?
